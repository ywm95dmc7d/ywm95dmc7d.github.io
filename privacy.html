Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Trip Ledger is an iOS app for organizing trip expenses, saving receipts, and documenting rental vehicle condition. This policy explains what information the app uses and how you control it.

Yahoo Mail is not connected yet. Yahoo receipt search is planned, pending Yahoo’s developer access approval. The app does not access Yahoo mail unless that feature is later approved, added to the app, and you choose to connect an account.

Information you save

Trip Ledger stores trip names, destinations and dates; expense amounts, categories, merchants and notes; receipt images and email attachments you choose to save; and rental company, vehicle, license plate, VIN, odometer, fuel, pickup time, checklist status, notes and inspection photos that you enter or capture.

The app can use your device camera or photo library when you choose to take or add a receipt or vehicle photo. You can also choose an attachment from a receipt email to add to an expense.

Email connections

When you ask Trip Ledger to search Gmail or Outlook/Hotmail, you authorize its read-only connection to search your mailbox for likely receipt messages. The app shows possible matches for you to review. It saves an expense and selected attachment only when you choose to import a message. Trip Ledger does not send, delete, or modify email and does not store your mailbox password. Google and Microsoft handle sign-in under their own privacy terms.

Yahoo Mail access is pending developer approval and is not currently available. If a Yahoo connector is later added, it will be described here before release. The intended feature is user-requested receipt search and import using read-only access.

Storage and sync

Trip Ledger stores your records in its local app database. When iCloud is available and enabled, the app is configured to sync records using the private iCloud database associated with your Apple Account. If the iCloud-backed store cannot start, the app attempts to use local-only storage and warns that records may not sync to other devices. You can export a JSON backup to Files.

Trip Ledger does not currently use an app-operated server to store trip records. If you choose to export or share a report or backup, the destination app and service you select handle that file under their own terms and privacy practices.

How information is used

Your trip information is used to display expenses, totals and categories, save receipt evidence, create rental inspection records, and generate reports or backups you request. Mailbox access is used only for the receipt-search feature that you start.

Trip Ledger does not sell your information or use it for advertising targeting. It does not use mailbox data to build advertising profiles or engagement segments.

Retention and deletion

Records remain until you delete them. You can remove an individual expense or use Connections → Delete all trips and inspections to remove saved trips, expenses, receipts and inspections in the app. When iCloud sync is active, deletions are synced to your other devices. You can use Disconnect in Connections to remove a saved mailbox sign-in from Trip Ledger. You can also revoke access in your Google or Microsoft account settings.

Backups exported to Files are under your control and are not automatically deleted when you delete in-app records.

Your choices

You decide whether to connect an email account, which messages and attachments to import, which photos to capture or add, and whether to export or share a report. You can disconnect email accounts and delete saved records from within the app.

Contact

Trip Ledger does not publish a public support email address. A public support contact will be added before a public App Store release. The developer contact address is provided privately in the Yahoo developer access request and is not displayed on this website.
